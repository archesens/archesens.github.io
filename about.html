THE COMPANY BEHIND ARCHESENS

Thoughtful technology.
For indoor spaces.

ARCHESENS PTE. LTD. is a Singapore company developing sensing technology and connected products for the spaces where people live and work.

WHAT WE DO

01

From practical questions
to product development.

Our work brings together sensing technology, product design and software. Our goal is to help indoor spaces become more aware of their conditions and more useful to the people within them.

CookMind brings this direction to our first application: everyday cooking. It is being developed as a Cooking Awareness Companion. Our broader company vision extends across indoor spaces.

ArcheSens is our company brand; CookMind is the product brand. Both are part of the work of ARCHESENS PTE. LTD.

WHY WE STARTED

02

A personal experience
became a starting point.

Our founder experienced how easily work and family responsibilities could draw attention away from cooking. That experience prompted a lasting interest in making everyday cooking awareness more useful.

It is the motivation behind CookMind. Product development, testing and clear communication of limitations are part of turning that motivation into something people can use.

OUR PHILOSOPHY · SENSE. UNDERSTAND. ACT.

Sense.

Begin with awareness of the conditions and changes within an indoor space.

Understand.

Explore how context can turn observations into information that matters to people.

Act.

Make understanding useful: support informed decisions and thoughtful responses in everyday life.

These principles guide our development. Each product’s capabilities and limitations are established through its own design and testing.
