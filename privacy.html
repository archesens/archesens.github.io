WEBSITE INFORMATION

Website privacy.

This page describes the company website for ARCHESENS PTE. LTD. It does not describe the data practices of the CookMind product or its associated services.

Browsing this website

These pages provide information about our company and product development. The website code does not include advertising trackers, analytics tools, account registration, newsletter subscriptions or contact forms. Images, styles and scripts used by these pages are served with the website.

Contacting us by email

Email links open your email application. If you choose to write to us, your email address and the information you include in your message are used to handle your enquiry and any follow-up correspondence. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or other information that is not needed for your enquiry.

Technical service information

Delivering a website or an email can involve processing technical information, such as an IP address, request details or email routing information, by the services used for delivery. The absence of website analytics does not mean that these services process no data.

Product information

CookMind is in development. Privacy information for the product and its associated services will be provided separately for the applicable release.

Questions

For questions about this website or information you have sent to us, contact support@archesens.com.
